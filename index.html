Methodology

How we calculate your aggregate

The industry-standard formula used across Rivers State's tertiary institutions.

01 · FORMULA

Each school, its own formula

RSU: (JAMB ÷ 8) + Post-UTME, where Post-UTME is scored out of 50 — added directly, not halved.
UNIPORT: (JAMB + Post-UTME) ÷ 8, with Post-UTME scored out of 400 like JAMB.
IAUE: runs no separate Post-UTME test — your JAMB score alone (÷ 4) is your aggregate.

02 · BENCHMARKS

General entry floors

RSU requires a minimum JAMB score of 190 to sit Post-UTME. UNIPORT's floor is 160. IAUE varies by course, from 140 up to 250 for the most competitive programmes.

03 · ZONES

Merit, catchment & risk

We compare your aggregate against each department's published or estimated cut-off to place you in a Merit, Catchment, or Change-of-Course risk zone.

⚠️

Important: Departmental cut-offs, especially UNIPORT's, are not always officially published and can shift year to year based on applicant volume and admission quotas. RSU's official calculation has also been cited inconsistently — some sessions divide Post-UTME by 2, others don't. Figures here are compiled from public admission-guide sources for guidance only. Always confirm your final aggregate and department status on your school's official admission portal before making decisions.
